I The Roadmap Principle
"
Why This Roadmap Is Sequenced This Way
You cannot monetise a product users don't trust. You cannot scale a product that isn't worth returning to. Trust first. Value second. Scale third. Every sequencing decision in this roadmap flows from that order.

V The Saransh Dependency — Managing the Critical Path
"
Strategic Dependency — Why Two Roadmap Items Are Blocked
Saransh is not a feature. It is the data infrastructure that unlocks Rajniti's highest-potential roadmap items. The right PM decision is not to force-build News vs Politician on weak data — it is to invest in Saransh first so both features ship with strong confidence and no credibility risk.
What Saransh does
Categorises daily Indian civic news and maps each story to the accountable politician
What it unlocks in Rajniti
News vs Politician mapping + Leaderboards. RICE scores jump from ~60K to ~107K when unblocked
Dependency management
Bi-weekly check-in with Saransh team lead. Clear integration spec agreed by end of Q1.
VI The Three Trade-offs This Roadmap Makes Explicitly
Trade-off 1
Trust before revenue
Source References ships before AI Performance Summary despite having a higher RICE score. A trustworthy product with fewer features retains better than an impressive product users do not trust. In civic tech, one viral wrong data point ends the product. We buy the trust moat first.
Trade-off 2
Scale before B2B contracts
B2B outreach starts in Q1 but contracts are a Q2 KR. Forcing B2B contracts at 10K users produces bad deals at low prices. Waiting until 200K MAU gives Rajniti pricing power and genuine leverage. Q1 builds the pipeline. Q2 closes it.
Trade-off 3
English-first, then Hindi
Hindi localisation unlocks 2× the addressable market but sits in Q3. Localising an unproven product is expensive and reversible. English-first lets us validate the core product loop — research → trust → upgrade — before investing in the translation layer. If the loop breaks, localisation is wasted effort.
Trade-off 4
Defer leaderboards until Saransh ships
Politician Leaderboards have high viral potential but a 50% confidence score on election data alone. Publishing a "Most Corrupt Politicians" list built on incomplete data is a reputational catastrophe. We wait for Saransh's news categorisation pipeline to raise confidence to 85% before shipping.
