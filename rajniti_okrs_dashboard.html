I North Star Metric — The One Number That Matters
★
North Star Metric
Verified Civic Research Sessions per Month
A session where a user views a politician profile AND spends 60+ seconds — evidence of genuine research, not an accidental click. Not raw sessions. Not page views. Not logins. The specific action that proves Rajniti is fulfilling its civic mission.
Why 60 seconds: below this threshold, the user is scanning, not researching. A voter who reads an AI performance summary, checks attendance records, and scrolls through asset declarations will always exceed 60 seconds. One who opens a profile by mistake will not.
II Q1 2025 OKR — Foundation Quarter
Q1 2025 · Jan → Mar · Foundation Quarter
Establish Rajniti as the most trusted civic research tool for Indian voters — before the Bihar election cycle begins.
Trust + Scale
KR1
Reach 100,000 Monthly Active Users by end of Q1
100K is 0.43% of SAM — conservative enough to be credible, meaningful enough to generate word-of-mouth. Below 50K, growth signal is too weak. Above 200K without paid acquisition requires an unlikely viral event. 70% success = 70,000 MAU.
100K
MAU
KR2
Achieve 8% free-to-premium conversion among users who complete 3+ politician profile views
Conversion on all users is a vanity metric — most churn before experiencing the product. Conversion among activated users (3+ profile views) measures whether the premium value proposition is landing with people who've actually used the product. This is the real signal.
8%
Conversion
KR3
Achieve 100% source attribution across all politician data points in the app
Source attribution is not a feature — it is the trust foundation the entire product rests on. If this is not at 100% before we scale, we are growing on a broken foundation. This KR gates everything else. A single unattributed data point that surfaces in a viral screenshot can define Rajniti's reputation permanently.
100%
Attribution
KR4
Secure 3 warm B2B pipeline conversations with NGOs or media organisations
Not contracts — conversations. At 100K users, Rajniti is not yet large enough to command ₹50K/month from B2B buyers. The Q1 goal is to build the pipeline: identify the 10 target organisations, open conversations with 3, and understand their data needs. Contracts come in Q2 once scale justifies the ask.
3
B2B Leads
⚡
Why Q1 B2B target is conversations, not contracts
B2B only works at scale. A media house does not pay ₹50,000/month for API access to a product with 10,000 users. They pay when Rajniti has 200,000+ users and the data consumption is clearly validated. Q1 is about building trust and user base. Q2 is when the B2B ask becomes credible. Writing a Q1 KR for "5 B2B contracts" would be sandbagging the real goal — user growth — in favour of a revenue metric we cannot yet earn.
