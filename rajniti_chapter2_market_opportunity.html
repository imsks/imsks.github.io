I The Market Argument — Two Problems, One Product
Problem One — The Supply Gap
The data exists. Nobody has made it usable.
Every Indian MP and MLA's attendance record, voting history, asset declaration, and criminal disclosure is publicly filed with the Election Commission. It is all technically accessible. None of it is structured, synthesised, or presented in a form a first-time voter can use in 5 minutes. The raw data sits in PDFs. Rajniti's job is to close the last mile between public data and public understanding.
Problem Two — The Trust Gap
The information that does circulate is unverified.
The primary source of political information for most Indian voters is WhatsApp. Politically motivated, unattributed, and actively misleading. The products that do have verified data — MyNeta, ADR India — are built for researchers and journalists, not voters. Rajniti sits at the intersection of verified data and consumer-grade UX. That intersection is currently empty. That is the opportunity.

IV Pricing Architecture — The Dual Revenue Model
The Core Pricing Tension
The users who benefit most from Rajniti are least able to pay for it. Charging voters undermines the civic mission and kills scale. The solution: keep the civic core permanently free, and monetise through institutional buyers — NGOs, media, and researchers — who derive commercial value from the voter base Rajniti builds. Free tier is not a loss leader. It is the asset that makes the B2B tier valuable.

The Pricing Decision That Could Kill the Business
Crippling the free tier to push upgrades. The moment the free tier stops being genuinely useful — when basic MP lookup requires a subscription, or citations are paywalled — Rajniti breaks the civic mission and loses the scale that makes the B2B data valuable. The free tier is not charity. It is the engine of the entire revenue model. If it stops being useful, the flywheel stops spinning.
V Why Now — The GTM Timing Argument

The Timing Argument — 2025 is the Right Year
India holds state elections every 6–8 months. Every election cycle is a built-in acquisition spike — civic research intent spikes in the 6 weeks before polling day. Launch in a pre-election window and the product markets itself. No other category has this structural growth trigger.
Bihar / Delhi elections 2025 TBE 100K seed audience ready AI infrastructure costs at historic low Trust in WhatsApp political info at historic low No well-funded competitor in white space
VI The Positioning Statement
One Sentence. Every Strategic Decision Flows From This.
For first-time and civically engaged Indian voters who want to evaluate their MP or MLA's verified track record — not campaign promises, Rajniti is the only civic research tool that combines election-commission-verified data with an AI synthesis layer accessible to any voter — unlike MyNeta, which holds the same data but requires research expertise to interpret, and unlike WhatsApp, which delivers political information with no source verification at all.
