The GTM Timing Argument — Why 2025 is the Right Year
India has state elections every 6–8 months. Rajniti's growth curve is directly tied to election cycles — each cycle is a built-in acquisition spike. Launch in a pre-election window and the product markets itself.
IV Why This Market Is Defensible — Rajniti's Moat
Data Moat
Verified political data no one else has structured
MP/MLA attendance, voting records, asset declarations, criminal cases — all public but unstructured. Rajniti's data pipeline is the product. A competitor can build the UX but not the data layer overnight.
Trust Moat
Source attribution in a misinformation-heavy space
Civic products live or die on trust. Rajniti's citation-first design builds a trust reputation that takes years to replicate — especially in a domain where one wrong data point causes reputational damage.
Community Moat
Civic network effect via The Boring Education
Rajniti can seed its first 100K users from TBE's 100K+ community — young, politically engaged, digitally native. No competitor can replicate this distribution channel. First-mover trust compounds.
V Assumption Audit — Every Number Defended
970M
Election Commission of India Voter List 2024 — the hardest number in this model. Government source, directly verifiable.
65%
TRAI Q4 2023: 900M+ active internet subscribers in India. 900M / 1.4B population ≈ 64%. Rounded to 65% applied to voter-age population.
25%
Reuters Institute Digital News Report India 2023: ~26% of Indian online adults actively seek news vs. passively receive it. Rounded to 25%.
15%
Conservative proxy: ~15% of civic news consumers use dedicated political fact-checking or analysis apps. Harder to source — flagged as weakest assumption.
8%
Free-to-paid benchmark: NDTV premium conversion ~7–9%. Inshorts premium tier similar. ₹99/month is below the psychological barrier that kills civic app upgrades.
₹50K
B2B API pricing: benchmarked against PRS Legislative Research and ADR India's data licensing fees to media partners. ₹50K/month is mid-market for civic data API access.
1.5%
Base SOM: comparable civic apps in India (MyNeta, Voterhelp) achieved ~1–2% of their SAM in Year 1 pre-elections. 1.5% is mid-range.
25 orgs
Base B2B: India has 50+ election-focused NGOs, 10+ political desks at national media houses, and 20+ academic centres studying Indian democracy. 25 is a 25% capture of known pipeline.
