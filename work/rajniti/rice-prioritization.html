The Strategic Override — When RICE is Right and Wrong Simultaneously
Source References scores highest (634,500) because it is low effort and affects every user. AI Performance Summary scores second (282,000) because of its higher effort cost. But AI Performance Summary ships first. Here is why: Source References makes the product trustworthy. AI Performance Summary makes the product worth paying for. A product that is trustworthy but not valuable enough to upgrade has no revenue. A product with a compelling premium tier but weak trust signals has no retention. The correct sequence: build the trust layer (Source References, Sprint 1) then immediately follow with the value layer (AI Summary, Sprint 1–2 overlap). RICE ranks them correctly in isolation. Strategic sequencing determines which ships first in a resource-constrained sprint.
